## Crosswind vs. Decision Matrix
- If crosswind > 75% of limit: Go-around recommended
- If crosswind > 90% of limit: Diversion to alternate airport
- Gusts: If gust factor > 10 knots above mean wind: add 5 knots to approach speed
- Wet runway: Effective crosswind limit reduces by 20% vs. dry runway values

## Wind Direction Reference for Crosswind Calculation
Crosswind Component = Wind Speed × sin(angle between wind and runway heading)
Headwind Component = Wind Speed × cos(angle between wind and runway heading)
