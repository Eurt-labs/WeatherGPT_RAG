# Cloud Ceiling & Visibility — IFR/VFR Thresholds
Source: ICAO Doc 8168, DGCA ANO Series X, AAI AIP India

## VFR vs. IFR Classification
| Condition | Ceiling | Visibility | Flight Rules |
|-----------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| VMC (Visual Meteorological Conditions) | > 1000 ft AGL | > 5000 m | VFR allowed |
| IMC (Instrument Meteorological Conditions) | ≤ 1000 ft AGL | ≤ 5000 m | IFR required |
| LIFR | < 500 ft AGL | < 1600 m | Limited IFR; advanced certification needed |

## Approach Categories & Minima (India)
| Category | RVR Minima | Decision Height | Certifications Required |
|---------|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| CAT I (Non-Precision) | 800 m RVR | 200 ft DH | Standard IFR |
| CAT I Precision | 550 m RVR | 200 ft DH | ILS approach |
| CAT II | 300 m RVR | 100 ft DH | Specialised crew + aircraft |
| CAT IIIA | 200 m RVR | 50 ft DH | Autoland systems |
| CAT IIIB | 50–200 m RVR | 15 ft or less DH | Fully autoland; Delhi, Mumbai certified |

## Indian Airports Certified for CAT III Operations
- Indira Gandhi International (Delhi — VIDP): CAT IIIB
- Chhatrapati Shivaji (Mumbai — VABB): CAT IIIB
- Kempegowda (Bengaluru — VOBL): CAT II
- Chennai (VOMM): CAT II
- (All other Indian airports: CAT I only)

## Monsoon Cloud Types Affecting Operations
| Cloud Type | Base Height | Flying Hazard |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Cumulonimbus (CB) | 500–2000 ft | Severe turbulence, hail, lightning, icing |
| Nimbostratus | < 3000 ft | Low ceiling, continuous rain, IMC |
| Stratocumulus | 1500–6000 ft | Reduced visibility, light icing at altitude |
| Cumulus congestus | 2000–15000 ft | Possible towering to CB; convective activity |

## Icing Levels for Indian Operations
- Icing risk: When visible moisture (cloud/rain) AND temp -20°C to +2°C
- Significant icing: Himalayan crossings during winter; W. Ghats during monsoon at FL150–200
- PIREP (Pilot Report) required for moderate or severe icing for AAI SIGMET
