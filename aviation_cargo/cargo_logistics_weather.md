# Cargo & Logistics Weather Impact Guide
Source: Indian Logistics Industry, MoRTH, Railway Board, DGCA Cargo Regulations

## Road Freight Weather Impact
| Condition | Road Freight Impact | Advisory |
|-----------|---------------------|---------|
| Rain > 30 mm/hr | Slowing on NH; hydroplaning risk | Reduce speed; add 1–2 hours transit time |
| Visibility < 200 m (fog) | Major delay risk on IGP routes | Allow 30–50% extra transit time; convoy travel |
| Heatwave > 45°C | Perishables: refrigerated vehicle mandatory | Check reefer temp every 2 hours |
| Flood / road closure | Detour via alternate NH | Track CWC water levels for route planning |
| Cyclone (Orange+) | Suspend operations in coastal districts | Pre-position stock before landfall |

## Rail Freight Disruptions
- Fog season (Dec–Jan): Goods trains delayed 2–8 hours in IGP zone
- Flood: Track closures in Bihar/Assam; average 24–72h disruption per event
- Cyclone: Coastal rail routes (Chennai–Vijayawada, Bhubaneswar corridor) suspended pre-landfall

## Air Cargo Weather Considerations
- Low visibility: Belly cargo operations continue; only charter cargo affected by diversions
- Temperature-sensitive cargo: Pharma (2–8°C), fresh produce (0–4°C): critical when ground temp > 35°C
  - Tarmac handling window: < 2 hours between AC storage and aircraft for pharma
  - Cold chain break threshold: Any temp exposure > 8°C for > 30 minutes = batch rejection
- Hail risk: Aircraft on ground with active hail alert → move to hangar or suspend loading

## Port & Coastal Cargo (Non-Aviation)
- Wind > 40 knots: Crane operations at ports suspended
- Wave height > 3 m: Small vessel operations in harbour approach suspended
- Cyclone Warning: Port clearance of all vessels; cargo operations stop 48h before expected landfall
- Fog: Port visibility < 500 m → pilotage operations restricted to daylight only

## Perishable Cargo Temperature Guidelines
| Commodity | Ideal Temp | Alert Temp | Transit Risk Month |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Fresh Vegetables | 0–4°C | > 8°C | May–June |
| Cut Flowers | 2–4°C | > 6°C | Apr–May |
| Pharmaceuticals | 2–8°C | > 15°C | Year-round |
| Frozen Seafood | -18°C | > -15°C | Year-round |
| Fresh Milk | 1–4°C | > 6°C | Apr–Sep |
