# Wind Shear & Turbulence — Aviation Weather
Source: ICAO, WMO Aviation Meteorology, DGCA SIGMET Guidelines

## Wind Shear Classification
| Type | Description | Risk |
|------|------------|------|
| Low-Level Wind Shear (LLWS) | Wind speed/direction change in lowest 2000 ft | Critical during takeoff/landing |
| Mountain Wave Turbulence | Oscillations downwind of Himalayas/Western Ghats | Moderate to severe turbulence |
| Clear Air Turbulence (CAT) | High-altitude (FL 200+) jet stream turbulence | Invisible; sudden onset |
| Convective Turbulence | Within/near thunderstorms | Severe to extreme |

## Turbulence Intensity Scale (ICAO)
| Intensity | Effect on Aircraft | Passenger Experience |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Light | Slight erratic control | Minor discomfort; seatbelt advisory |
| Moderate | Aircraft altitude/attitude changes difficult | Unsecured items displaced; difficult to walk |
| Severe | Large abrupt changes; control momentarily lost | Occupants thrown against restraints |
| Extreme | Aircraft practically uncontrollable | Structural damage possible |

## Thunderstorm Avoidance Rules (ICAO/DGCA)
- Avoid cumulonimbus (CB) by: 20 NM horizontally; 1000 ft above anvil
- Never fly between two active CBs if gap < 25 NM
- Do NOT penetrate CBs (severe hail, icing, turbulence, lightning)
- If unavoidable: reduce speed to turbulence penetration speed; auto-pilot off

## Indian Aviation Turbulence Hot Spots
| Location | Season | Type |
|----------|--------|------|
| Himalayan foothills | Oct–Mar | Mountain wave |
| Deccan Plateau convergence | Sep–Oct | Convective |
| Western Ghats | Jun–Sep | Orographic uplift + CB |
| Bay of Bengal approaches | Oct–Dec | Tropical thunderstorms |
| Rajasthan desert | May–Jun | Dry convective; dust devils |

## SIGMET Issuance Thresholds (India — AAI/IMD)
- Turbulence SIGMET issued when: moderate or greater turbulence affecting large area (>125,000 sq km)
- Wind shear SIGMET: LLWS > 8 knots/100 ft change reported by pilot or wind profiler
- Valid period: Max 4 hours; 6 hours for volcanic ash/tropical cyclone
