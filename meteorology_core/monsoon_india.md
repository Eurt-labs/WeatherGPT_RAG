# Indian Monsoon — Phases, Forecasting & Sectoral Impact
Source: IMD, Earth System Sciences, Monsoon Mission IITM Pune

## Southwest (Summer) Monsoon — Overview
- **Period**: June 1 – September 30 (approximately)
- **Direction**: Arabian Sea + Bay of Bengal → Inland India
- **Annual contribution**: ~80% of India's total annual rainfall
- **Governing System**: Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ) migration northward

## Monsoon Variability Indices
| Index | Description | Impact |
|-------|------------|--------|
| El Niño (ENSO+) | Warm SST in central Pacific | Below-normal Indian monsoon (drought risk) |
| La Niña (ENSO-) | Cool SST in central Pacific | Above-normal Indian monsoon |
| IOD+ (Positive Indian Ocean Dipole) | Warm W. Indian Ocean | Strengthens monsoon |
| IOD- (Negative) | Cool W. Indian Ocean | Weakens monsoon |
| MJO (Madden-Julian Oscillation) | 30–60 day wave | Active/break phases of monsoon |

## Monsoon Active & Break Phases
- **Active Monsoon**: Continuous rainfall across India; lasts 7–14 days
  - Trigger: Strong southwesterly winds; deep low-pressure systems over Bay
- **Monsoon Break**: Little or no rainfall; clear skies; drought-like conditions
  - Cause: Northward shift of ITCZ; increased northern ridge
  - Agricultural risk: If break lasts > 14 days during July–August = crop stress

## NE Monsoon (Winter Monsoon — Tamil Nadu & Andhra)
- Period: October 15 – December 31
- Affects: Tamil Nadu, coastal Andhra Pradesh, Southeast Odisha, Sri Lanka
- Tamil Nadu gets 35% of annual rainfall from NE monsoon
- Associated with cyclones in Bay of Bengal (Oct–Dec peak)

## Sectoral Impact Summary
| Sector | Impact of Good Monsoon | Impact of Deficient Monsoon |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Agriculture | High Kharif yield; good soil moisture for Rabi | Crop failure; farmer distress; loan waivers |
| Hydro Power | High reservoir levels; surplus power | Power cuts; load shedding |
| Urban Water Supply | Reservoir filling | Water rationing; tanker supply |
| Flood Zones | Flood events (excess rain areas) | Minimal flooding |
| Drought Zones | Relief | Intensified drought |
