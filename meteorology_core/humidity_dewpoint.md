# Humidity, Dew Point & Apparent Temperature Guide
Source: WMO, IMD, ASHRAE Comfort Standards

## Relative Humidity Interpretation
| RH % | Description | Human Comfort | Crop Impact |
|------|------------|--------------|------------|
| < 20% | Extremely Dry | Skin dryness; respiratory irritation | Severe moisture stress for most crops |
| 20–40% | Dry | Comfortable in cool temps | Rapid ET₀; sprinkler irrigation preferred |
| 40–60% | Comfortable | Ideal indoor comfort range | Optimal for most field crops |
| 60–75% | Humid | Comfortable if temp < 30°C | Monitor fungal diseases |
| 75–85% | Very Humid | Discomfort; sweating | High disease pressure (blast, powdery mildew) |
| > 85% | Oppressive | Heat stroke risk; discomfort | Spray ban; fungi/bacteria thrive |

## Dew Point Temperature — Key Thresholds
| Dew Point | Perceived Humidity |
|-----------|-------------------|
| < 10°C | Dry and comfortable |
| 10–15°C | Comfortable |
| 16–18°C | Slightly humid |
| 18–21°C | Humid; slightly uncomfortable |
| 21–24°C | Very humid; uncomfortable |
| > 24°C | Oppressive; heat stroke risk |

## Dew Point and Fog Formation
- Fog forms when air temperature drops to ≤ dew point
- Temperature – Dew Point Spread (T-Td):
  - T-Td < 2°C → Fog likely within hours
  - T-Td < 1°C → Fog imminent
  - T-Td = 0°C → Fog / saturated air

## Apparent Temperature (Feels-Like) Formula
At = T + 0.33 × (RH/100 × 6.105 × exp(17.27 × T/(237.7+T))) − 0.70 × wind_speed − 4.0

Key ranges:
- At > 40°C → Extreme heat advisory
- At 32–40°C → Heat caution
- At -5 to -15°C → Cold wind chill advisory
- At < -15°C → Frostbite risk within 30 minutes

## Crop Disease — Humidity Triggers
| Disease | Crop | RH Threshold | Temp Window |
|---------|------|-------------|-------------|
| Rice Blast | Paddy | > 85% | 24–28°C |
| Late Blight | Potato/Tomato | > 80% | 15–22°C |
| Powdery Mildew | Wheat/Mustard | 70–80% | 15–25°C |
| Downy Mildew | Grapes/Onion | > 85% | 10–20°C |
| Alternaria Blight | Mustard | > 75% | 20–25°C |
| Cotton Boll Rot | Cotton | > 80% | 28–35°C |

## Spray Decision Based on Humidity
- RH < 40%: Avoid spraying (rapid evaporation reduces efficacy by 50%+)
- RH 40–80%: Optimal spraying window
- RH > 85%: Delay spraying; risk of dilution and fungal spread
