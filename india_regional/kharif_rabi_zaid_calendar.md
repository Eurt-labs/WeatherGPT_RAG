# Kharif, Rabi & Zaid Seasonal Calendar — India
Source: ICAR, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, IMD Agro-met Division

## Season Overview
| Season | Sowing | Harvest | Main Trigger | Water Source |
|--------|--------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Kharif | May–July | Sep–November | SW Monsoon onset | Rainfall |
| Rabi | October–December | March–May | Monsoon withdrawal | Irrigation/Winter rain |
| Zaid | March–May | June–July | Post-Rabi gap season | Irrigation only |
