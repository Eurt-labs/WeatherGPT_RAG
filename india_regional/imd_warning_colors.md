# IMD Weather Warning Colour Code System
Source: IMD Standard Operating Procedure for Weather Warning; Updated 2021

## Four-Tier Warning System

| Colour | Alert Level | Required Action |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| GREEN | No Warning — Watch / Normal | Monitor regularly; no action needed |
| YELLOW | Watch — Be Updated | Stay alert; be aware of rapidly changing conditions |
| ORANGE | Alert — Be Prepared | Be prepared for disruption; ready emergency plans |
| RED | Warning — Take Action | Take immediate action; follow official orders |

## Warning Thresholds by Phenomenon

### Rainfall Warnings (24-hour)
| Condition | Threshold | Colour |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Heavy Rain | 64.5–115.5 mm | Orange |
| Very Heavy Rain | 115.6–204.4 mm | Red |
| Extremely Heavy Rain | > 204.5 mm | Red (Extreme) |

### Cyclone Warnings (Wind Speed at Landfall)
| Cyclone Type | Wind Speed | Warning Colour | Advance Notice |
|-------------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| Cyclonic Storm | 63–88 km/h | Yellow | T-5 days |
| Severe Cyclonic Storm | 89–117 km/h | Orange | T-3 days |
| Very Severe / Above | > 117 km/h | Red | T-24 to 48h (mandatory evacuation) |

### Heatwave Warnings
| Heat Level | Threshold | Colour |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Heat Wave | ≥ 40°C OR ≥ 4.5°C above normal | Yellow/Orange |
| Severe Heat Wave | ≥ 45°C OR ≥ 6.5°C above normal | Red |

### Cold Wave Warnings
| Cold Level | Threshold (Min Temp Departure) | Colour |
|-----------|-------------------------------|--------|
| Cold Wave | ≥ 4.5°C below normal for 2+ days | Yellow |
| Severe Cold Wave | ≥ 6.5°C below normal | Orange/Red |

### Fog Warnings
| Fog Type | Visibility | Colour |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Dense Fog | 50–200 m | Orange |
| Very Dense Fog | < 50 m | Red |

### Thunderstorm Warnings
| Type | Description | Colour |
|------|------------|--------|
| Thunderstorm | Lightning risk; gusty winds | Yellow (standard) |
| Severe Thunderstorm | Gusts > 60 km/h + heavy rain | Orange |
| Tornado / Squall | Violent; rare | Red |

## Sector-Specific IMD Alert Bulletins
- **Agro-Meteorological Advisory Service (AMAS)**: 5-day crop-specific district-level advice
- **Fishery Weather Forecast**: Daily; wind/wave warnings for Bay of Bengal / Arabian Sea
- **State-level District Warnings**: Issued 48h and 24h in advance for each district
- **City Forecast**: Hourly forecast for 450+ cities (IMD Mausam App)

## IMD Forecast Dissemination Channels
1. Doordarshan (TV): Weather bulletin 7:30 PM (national)
2. All India Radio: 6:30 AM, 7:30 PM
3. IMD Mausam App: Real-time nowcast + 7-day forecast
4. SMS Alerts: Via network operators during Red/Orange warnings
5. NDMA Portal: Integrated multi-hazard warning dashboard
