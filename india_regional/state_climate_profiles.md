# Indian State Climate Profiles — Weather Patterns & Sectoral Notes
Source: IMD State Climate Reports, Census 2011, MoA&FW, ICAR

## Major States — Climate Summary

### 1. Punjab & Haryana (Rabi Wheat Belt)
- Climate: Semi-arid; hot summers (46°C), cold winters (4°C)
- SW Monsoon: July–August (350–650 mm annual)
- Key Events: Dense fog (Dec–Jan), dust storms (Apr–May)
- Primary Crops: Wheat, Paddy, Mustard, Maize
- Key Risk: Waterlogging during excess monsoon; fog disrupts Rabi harvest transport
- Officer Alert: Punjab flood plains (Ghaggar, Beas rivers) at risk Aug–Sep

### 2. Uttar Pradesh (Most Agriculturally Diverse)
- Climate: Semi-arid to sub-humid; Terai zone sub-tropical
- Annual Rainfall: 650–1000 mm (W to E gradient)
- Key Events: Severe fog (Dec–Jan Agra/Varanasi), heatwaves (May–Jun), floods (Aug–Sep)
- Primary Crops: Wheat, Paddy, Sugarcane, Pulses, Potato
- Rivers: Ganga, Yamuna, Ghaghra, Rapti — all flood-prone in August

### 3. Rajasthan (Desert + Arid Agriculture)
- Climate: Arid (Thar Desert) to semi-arid (eastern Rajasthan)
- Annual Rainfall: 100–500 mm (W to E)
- Key Events: Dust storms (Andhi) May–Jun; extreme heat 48°C; flash floods in Barmer (surprisingly)
- Primary Crops: Bajra, Moth Bean, Jowar, Cluster Bean, Mustard (eastern Rajasthan)
- Drought: Chronic; MGNREGA activated almost every year in W Rajasthan

### 4. Maharashtra (Cotton + Sugarcane Belt)
- Climate: Western Ghats: heavy monsoon (3000–5000 mm); Marathwada: drought-prone
- Annual Rainfall: 500–5000 mm (huge gradient)
- Key Events: Marathwada drought; Mumbai floods; Western Ghats landslides (Jul–Aug)
- Primary Crops: Cotton, Sugarcane, Soybean, Grape, Orange, Onion
- Cyclone Risk: Arabian Sea cyclones (Jun, Oct–Nov) — Gujarat/Maharashtra coast

### 5. Madhya Pradesh (Soybean Capital)
- Climate: Sub-humid tropical; hot dry summers
- Annual Rainfall: 750–1500 mm
- Key Events: Excess monsoon (Aug) in Vindhya, Narmada zones; heatwaves in Gwalior/Bhopal
- Primary Crops: Soybean, Wheat, Chickpea, Maize, Cotton

### 6. Gujarat (Cotton + Groundnut)
- Climate: Semi-arid to arid; coastal influence in Saurashtra
- Annual Rainfall: 350–1500 mm
- Key Events: Cyclones (Arabian Sea); drought in Kutch; Narmada floods
- Primary Crops: Groundnut, Cotton, Wheat, Castor, Mango

### 7. Tamil Nadu (NE Monsoon Dependent)
- Climate: Hot tropical; coastal; two monsoon seasons
- Annual Rainfall: 900–1100 mm (60% from NE monsoon Oct–Dec)
- Key Events: Cyclones (Bay of Bengal Oct–Dec); Nilgiris foggy winters
- Primary Crops: Paddy (3 seasons), Banana, Coconut, Sugarcane, Mango

### 8. Kerala (Spice + Plantation State)
- Climate: Tropical wet; highest rainfall in India (3000–4000 mm on Ghats)
- Key Events: Landslides (Wayanad, Munnar Jul–Aug); coastal flooding; early monsoon onset
- Primary Crops: Rubber, Coconut, Tea, Coffee, Pepper, Cardamom

### 9. West Bengal & Odisha (Cyclone + Flood Prone)
- Climate: Humid tropical; heavy monsoon; deltaic flooding
- Key Events: Bay of Bengal cyclones (Oct–Dec); Brahmaputra/Ganga floods (Aug–Sep)
- Primary Crops: Paddy, Jute, Tea (Darjeeling), Potato

### 10. Bihar (Most Flood Vulnerable)
- Climate: Sub-humid; Himalayan river flooding
- Annual Rainfall: 1000–1500 mm
- Key Disasters: Kosi river floods (worst in India by frequency); waterlogging
- Primary Crops: Paddy, Wheat, Maize, Litchi, Makhana (foxnut)

### 11. Assam & Northeast (High Rainfall + Floods)
- Climate: Subtropical humid; monsoon June–September
- Annual Rainfall: 2500–4000+ mm
- Key Events: Brahmaputra flooding (Jul–Aug); cyclones; cloudbursts
- Primary Crops: Tea, Rice, Jute, Mustard

### 12. Himachal Pradesh & Uttarakhand (Mountain States)
- Climate: Alpine; snowfall Oct–April at higher altitudes
- Key Events: Western Disturbances (Dec–Mar); GLOFs; cloudbursts (Jul–Aug)
- Primary Crops: Apple, Cherry, Potato, Wheat, Maize
