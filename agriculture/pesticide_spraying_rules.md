# Pesticide Spraying Weather Rules
Source: CIB&RC guidelines, ICAR-NCIPM, CIBRC Label Restrictions

## Core Weather Thresholds for Spraying

| Parameter | Safe Window | Unsafe — Do NOT Spray |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| Wind Speed | < 8 km/h (< 2.2 m/s) | > 15 km/h: high drift risk |
| Relative Humidity | 40–80% | < 40%: rapid evaporation; > 85%: fungal disease risk |
| Air Temperature | 15–32°C | > 35°C: rapid breakdown + volatilization |
| Rain | No rain for 4–6 hours post-spray | Rain within 1 hour washes off contact pesticides |
| Dew | No heavy dew on leaves | Dew dilutes and spreads contact herbicides |
| Inversion | Avoid low inversion (< 1000 ft) | Cold clear nights = spray inversion = drift |

## Best Spraying Time Windows
- **Morning**: 6:00–10:00 AM — cool temps, calm winds, stomata open
- **Evening**: 4:00–7:00 PM — reduced evaporation, moderate humidity
- **Avoid**: 11 AM – 3 PM (peak heat, maximum evaporation loss)

## Crop-Specific Spray Conditions

### Rice (Paddy)
- Blast fungicide: Spray at 25–28°C, humidity < 80%, calm winds
- BPH (Brown Plant Hopper): Spray into canopy base; requires low-volume high-pressure
- Avoid spraying 3 days before/after heavy rainfall (> 25 mm/day)

### Wheat
- Aphid control: Effective at 15–22°C; spray in morning when dew has dried
- Rust fungicide: Apply at first sign; 18–25°C optimal for spray retention
- Pre-harvest interval: Minimum 14 days before harvest for systemic pesticides

### Cotton
- Bollworm spray: 25–32°C, humidity 50–75%
- Whitefly: Prefer evening sprays; avoid spraying when daytime max > 38°C
- Avoid spraying 4 hours before expected rainfall

### Vegetables
- Fungicide: Apply within 24 hours before rain forecast for protectant action
- Insecticide: Morning application preferred; avoid pollinator activity (9 AM–5 PM for bees)

## Drone Spraying Rules (DGCA Guidelines)
- Wind: < 5 m/s for agricultural drones
- Visibility: > 500 m
- Altitude: 2–3 m above crop canopy
- No spraying during foggy conditions or near water bodies (within 50 m)
- Permission required for area > 500 acres/day in restricted airspace zones
