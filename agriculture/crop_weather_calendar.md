# Crop Weather Calendar — India
Source: ICAR, IMD Agro-met Division, Krishi Vigyan Kendra guidelines

## Indian Cropping Seasons
| Season | Months | Key Crops | Rainfall Need |
|--------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Kharif | Jun–Nov | Paddy, Cotton, Maize, Soybean, Groundnut, Bajra, Arhar | 600–1500 mm SW Monsoon |
| Rabi   | Nov–Apr | Wheat, Mustard, Barley, Chickpea, Lentil, Peas | 20–50 mm + irrigation |
| Zaid   | Apr–Jun | Watermelon, Cucumber, Moong, Bitter Gourd | Fully irrigation-dependent |

## Month-by-Month Weather Advisory

### January (Rabi vegetative stage)
- Temps: 5–22°C North Plains; 15–28°C South
- FROST RISK: Night temps < 4°C for 2+ consecutive nights → apply light pre-dawn irrigation to protect wheat/mustard
- Wheat tillering needs 70–80 mm water; irrigate every 20–25 days
- Chickpea: avoid waterlogging; 1 irrigation at branching stage only
- Mustard pollination: humidity > 80% increases Alternaria blight risk

### February (Rabi heading/flowering)
- Temps: 8–26°C North
- Wheat heading stage: last irrigation 60–65 days after sowing
- Dense fog (visibility < 200 m) delays field operations for 3–5 days in Punjab/Haryana
- Powdery mildew risk on mustard/wheat if humidity > 70% with mild temps (15–20°C)
- Begin Zaid land preparation in South India

### March (Rabi ripening/harvest)
- Temps: 15–32°C North; 22–38°C South
- Heat stress threshold: temp > 35°C during wheat grain filling → 2–5% yield loss per degree above
- Stop wheat irrigation after dough stage (75–80 days after sowing)
- Harvest mustard before temps > 38°C to prevent shattering
- Pre-monsoon thunderstorms begin in NE India — protect stored Rabi produce

### April (Rabi harvest complete; Zaid active)
- Temps: 22–40°C North; 28–42°C South/West
- Complete Rabi harvest before pre-monsoon showers (target field moisture < 14%)
- Zaid crops: irrigate every 5–7 days (ET₀ = 6–8 mm/day in April)
- Heatwave (> 40°C for 3+ days): increase irrigation frequency; use mulch to reduce soil temp
- Start paddy nursery preparation (bed raising) in coastal Karnataka, Tamil Nadu

### May (Pre-Kharif preparation)
- Temps: 28–46°C North; 30–44°C South
- ET₀ peaks at 8–12 mm/day — maximum irrigation demand across all crops
- Pre-monsoon thunderstorms in Kerala (May 20+): risk of fruit drop in mango orchards
- Paddy nursery raising: Punjab/Haryana target 15–31 May window
- Apply pre-emergent herbicide in Kharif-prepared fields before onset

### June (Kharif sowing begins)
- SW Monsoon onset: Kerala avg 1 Jun ± 7 days → NW India by 1 Jul
- Wait for 50–75 mm cumulative rainfall before paddy transplanting
- Cotton sowing: 1–15 June in Maharashtra/Gujarat; soil temp > 20°C required
- Bajra/Sorghum sowing at first good shower (> 25 mm in 2 days)
- Waterlogging risk: avoid sowing in low-lying fields without 30 cm drainage furrows

### July (Kharif vegetative peak)
- Peak monsoon; 200–400 mm/month across most regions
- Paddy: maintain 2–5 cm standing water; drain and re-flood every 3–4 days for SRI method
- Excess rain (> 150 mm/week): root rot, collar rot risk in groundnut and soybean
- Apply NPK fertilizer (top dressing) only after a dry spell of 2+ days
- Cotton bollworm: risk HIGH when humidity > 75% and temp 25–30°C

### August (Kharif reproductive stage)
- Monsoon break weeks common in North India
- Rice blast disease: optimum infection at 25–28°C with 85–90% relative humidity and leaf wetness > 10 hours
- Arhar/Tur: flowering stage; waterlogging reduces pod set by 30–40%; ensure field drainage
- Groundnut: pegging and pod formation; 40–50 mm moisture per 10-day period needed
- Cotton: boll formation; requires 4–5 days dry period between irrigations

### September (Kharif harvest begins; Rabi prep)
- Monsoon withdrawal NW India: avg 15–17 September
- Soybean harvest: grain moisture < 15% for safe storage; use thresher within 3 days of harvesting
- Begin Rabi tillage: 2 deep ploughings + 1 planking; soil moisture at 50–60% field capacity
- Paddy maturity check: 80% tillers should show golden-yellow colour before harvest
- Arhar harvest (short-duration varieties) in South India

### October (Rabi sowing window opens)
- NE Monsoon onset (Tamil Nadu/Andhra coast): avg 15–20 October
- Optimum wheat sowing dates: Oct 25 – Nov 15 (North India); Nov 1–25 (Central India)
- Mustard sowing: Sep 25 – Oct 15 in Rajasthan; Oct 1–20 in Punjab/Haryana
- Apply basal fertilizer NPK 120:60:40 kg/ha for wheat at sowing
- Cyclonic disturbances in Bay of Bengal — risk of crop lodging in coastal paddy

### November (Rabi vegetative; paddy harvest continues)
- Temps dropping: 8–24°C North India
- Wheat germination needs soil temp 20–25°C; below 10°C delays emergence by 2–3 weeks
- Dense fog begins from late November in IGP (Indo-Gangetic Plain)
- Complete Kharif harvest (cotton, maize) before fog season reduces sunlight
- Chickpea/Gram needs dry weather for germination; avoid sowing in wet soil

### December (Rabi tillering; winter crops active)
- Night temps: 4–14°C North India
- Wheat tillering critical: apply 1st top-dress nitrogen (30 kg N/ha) at 30–35 DAS
- Frost events: apply light evening irrigation (2–3 cm) to prevent ice crystal damage on leaves
- Mustard: check for aphid infestation when temps are 10–15°C with low wind
- Minimum sunshine hours 3/day required for pod development in Rabi oilseeds
