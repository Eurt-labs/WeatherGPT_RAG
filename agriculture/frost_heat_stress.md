# Frost & Heat Stress Thresholds for Indian Crops
Source: ICAR, IMD Cold Wave/Heatwave Bulletins, NABARD Crop Insurance norms

## Cold Wave & Frost Definitions (IMD)
- **Cold Wave**: When minimum temperature departs 4.5°C or more below normal for 2+ days
- **Severe Cold Wave**: Departure of 6.5°C or more below normal
- **Frost**: When minimum temperature at ground level falls to 0°C or below

## Crop Frost Damage Thresholds

| Crop | Frost Damage Threshold | Critical Stage |
|------|----------------------|----------------|
| Wheat | < 0°C (canopy temp) | Tillering and heading |
| Mustard | < -2°C | Flowering stage — total crop loss possible |
| Peas | < -3°C | Pod filling |
| Chickpea | < -4°C | Vegetative stage tolerant; flowering intolerant |
| Potato | < -1.5°C | Tuber initiation |
| Tomato | < 2°C | Fruit set |

## Frost Protection Measures
- **Pre-dawn irrigation**: Apply 2–3 cm of water evening before frost night
  (Water releases latent heat as it freezes, keeping crop temp above 0°C)
- **Smoke method**: Traditional smudge pots or biomass burning upwind
- **Wind machines**: Effective when inversion layer exists (temp increases 3–5°C with height)
- **Threshold**: If forecast minimum < 3°C, trigger frost advisory for wheat/mustard farmers

## Heat Wave & High-Temperature Stress

### IMD Heatwave Thresholds
- **Plains**: Max temp ≥ 40°C; OR departure ≥ 4.5°C above normal
- **Hilly regions**: Max temp ≥ 30°C
- **Severe Heatwave**: Max temp ≥ 45°C; OR departure ≥ 6.5°C above normal

### Crop Heat Stress Thresholds

| Crop | Threshold Temp | Effect |
|------|---------------|--------|
| Wheat | > 35°C during grain filling | 3–4% yield loss per degree above 35°C |
| Maize | > 36°C at silking/tasseling | Pollen viability drops > 50% above 36°C |
| Cotton | > 40°C continuous | Flower abscission; boll drop |
| Chickpea | > 33°C at flowering | Pod abortion |
| Tomato | > 35°C (day) / > 22°C (night) | Poor fruit set |
| Paddy | > 35°C at anthesis | Spikelet sterility increases sharply |

## Heat Stress Mitigation for Farmers
- Schedule irrigation at dawn (4–6 AM) and late evening (7–9 PM) during heatwaves
- Apply kaolin clay foliar spray (5%) to reflect solar radiation on leaf surface
- Use shade nets (25–40% shading) for vegetable crops when temp > 38°C
- Apply mulch (paddy straw, plastic) to keep soil temp below 30°C
