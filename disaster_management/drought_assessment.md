# Drought Assessment Framework — India
Source: IMD, CWC, NDMA, National Drought Management Authority

## IMD Rainfall Deficiency Classification
| Category | Rainfall Deficiency from Normal | Season |
|----------|---------------------------------|--------|
| Normal | ± 19% | — |
| Deficient | -20% to -59% | Drought Watch |
| Large Deficiency | -60% or more | Severe Drought |
| Excess | +20% to +59% | Flood Watch |
| Large Excess | +60% or more | Flood Warning |

## Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI) for Drought
| SPI Value | Category |
|-----------|---------|
| ≥ 2.0 | Extremely Wet |
| 1.5–1.99 | Very Wet |
| 1.0–1.49 | Moderately Wet |
| -0.99 to 0.99 | Near Normal |
| -1.0 to -1.49 | Moderately Dry |
| -1.5 to -1.99 | Severely Dry |
| ≤ -2.0 | Extremely Dry (Severe Drought) |

## Drought-Prone Zones in India
| Zone | States | Type of Drought |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Zone 1: Chronically Drought-Prone | Rajasthan, Gujarat (interior), Marathwada | Meteorological + Agricultural |
| Zone 2: Severely Drought-Prone | Parts of AP, Karnataka, TN | Agricultural |
| Zone 3: Moderately Drought-Prone | MP, Odisha interiors, Jharkhand | Agricultural + Hydrological |

## Agricultural Drought Indicators
- Soil moisture < PWP (Permanent Wilting Point) for > 5 consecutive days = crop stress
- ET deficit > 30% of crop water requirement = yield reduction threshold
- Paddy: 10 days without rain during tillering/heading = 20–40% yield loss

## NDMA Drought Relief Protocol
- Trigger: 2+ months of rainfall deficiency + SPI < -1.5
- Actions: MGNREGA work activation; fodder camps for livestock; water tanker deployment
- Crop insurance: PM Fasal Bima Yojana triggered at district level with IMD certification
- Ex-gratia payment: State-level after official drought declaration by government
