# Evacuation & Emergency Checklists — NDRF/SDRF SOPs
Source: NDMA, NDRF, Ministry of Home Affairs Disaster Response Guidelines

## Colour Code Warning → Evacuation Decision Matrix
| IMD Alert | Population Action | District Admin Action |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------------|
| Green | No action | Monitor |
| Yellow | Aware; keep essentials ready | Activate EOC; brief NDRF teams |
| Orange | Prepare for evacuation; move elderly/disabled | Pre-position boats; open shelters |
| Red | Mandatory evacuation from flood plains, coastal zones | Deploy NDRF; traffic management; press advisory |

## Farmer Emergency Checklist (Before Evacuation)
- [ ] Harvest all mature standing crops (if time permits)
- [ ] Secure livestock on elevated ground or community shelter
- [ ] Store seeds/inputs in waterproof bags at elevated location
- [ ] Photograph standing crops (for crop insurance claim)
- [ ] Take land records/Kisan Credit Card/Aadhaar documents
- [ ] Switch off electric pump sets; secure transformer connections
- [ ] Inform Gram Pradhan and Patwari before leaving

## District Officer Checklist (Flood Event)
- [ ] Activate District Emergency Operations Centre (EOC) at T-48h
- [ ] Communicate with Block Development Officers for headcount
- [ ] Deploy NDRF teams to flood-prone blocks with full rescue kit
- [ ] Pre-position food packets, ORS, dry rations at relief camps
- [ ] Coordinate with state power corporation for safe line shutdowns
- [ ] Activate emergency helpline: State DM helpline (1077)
- [ ] Daily 6-hourly reporting to State EOC

## Post-Disaster Relief Camp Standards (NDMA)
- Space: Minimum 3.5 sq m per person in covered shelter
- Water: 15 litres/person/day minimum (WHO standard)
- Sanitation: 1 toilet per 20 persons; separate for men/women
- Medical: 1 doctor per 500 persons; 1 nurse per 150
- Food: 450g cereal + 100g pulses + vegetables per person per day
- Vulnerable: Separate space for pregnant women, infants, elderly, disabled
