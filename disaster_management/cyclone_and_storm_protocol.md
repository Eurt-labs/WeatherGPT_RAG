# Cyclone & Severe Storm Protocol — India
Source: IMD Cyclone Warning Division, NDMA, INCOIS

## IMD Cyclone Categories
| Category | Wind Speed | Storm Surge | Advisory |
|----------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Cyclonic Storm | 63–88 km/h | 1–2 m | Yellow Alert |
| Severe Cyclonic Storm | 89–117 km/h | 2–3 m | Orange Alert |
| Very Severe Cyclonic Storm | 118–167 km/h | 3–4 m | Red Alert |
| Extremely Severe | 168–221 km/h | 4–6 m | Red Alert Extreme |
| Super Cyclonic Storm | > 222 km/h | > 6 m | Catastrophic |

## Bay of Bengal vs. Arabian Sea Season
| Ocean | Peak Season | Typical States Affected |
|-------|------------|------------------------|
| Bay of Bengal | Oct–Dec (post-monsoon); May–Jun (pre-monsoon) | Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Odisha, WB |
| Arabian Sea | June (onset); Oct–Nov (retreat) | Gujarat, Maharashtra, Kerala, Goa |

## IMD Warning Timeline
- T-5 days: Cyclone Watch issued; fishermen advisory
- T-3 days: Cyclone Warning issued; evacuation planning starts
- T-24 hours: Destructive Wind Warning; mandatory coastal evacuation
- T-6 hours: Landfall imminent advisory; shelters activated

## Farmer Advisory During Cyclone Watch
- Harvest all mature crops (paddy, banana, vegetables) immediately
- Tie/stake all orchard trees and banana plantations
- Store all harvested produce in elevated dry warehouses
- Do NOT spray pesticides 48 hours before expected landfall

## Storm Surge Risk by Coast
- Odisha coast: Highest vulnerability (flat coastal plains, dense population)
- Andhra Pradesh: Very high; large delta districts (Krishna, Godavari)
- Tamil Nadu: Moderate; cyclones weaken before landfall here more often
- Gujarat: High; Gulf of Khambhat amplifies storm surge

## Pre-Cyclone Checklist for Officers (NDRF SOP)
1. Activate District EOC at T-72 hours
2. Pre-position NDRF teams by T-48 hours
3. Coordinate with district collectors for evacuation vehicles
4. Open cyclone shelters (min. 1 shelter per 5,000 coastal population)
5. Activate mobile medical teams at T-24 hours
6. Maintain emergency power backup at all shelters
