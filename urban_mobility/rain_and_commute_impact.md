# Rain & Commute Impact — Indian Cities
Source: BIS, MoRTH, Smart Cities Mission, IMD Urban Flash Flood Guidelines

## Rainfall Thresholds vs. Urban Disruption Levels

| Rainfall (mm/hr) | Traffic Impact | Advisory |
|-----------------|---------------|---------|
| < 5 | Minimal | Normal commute |
| 5–15 | Moderate slowdown | Allow extra 15 min |
| 15–30 | Significant congestion | Avoid non-essential travel; use metro/bus |
| 30–50 | Major disruption | Underpasses/low-lying roads may flood |
| > 50 | Emergency | Do NOT drive; severe flash flood risk |

## Metro/Rail Disruption Thresholds
- Local train suspension: Visibility < 50m OR track flooding > 15 cm
- Metro delay: Usually unaffected by rain unless station flooding
- Long-distance trains: Delays begin when track sections waterlogged

## Flight Disruption (Urban Airport Context)
- Visibility < 600m → ILS approach only (CAT I); delays possible
- Visibility < 300m → CAT II; limited aircraft types
- Crosswind > 25 knots (46 km/h) → potential diversions at smaller airports
