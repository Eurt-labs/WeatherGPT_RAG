# Fog & Visibility — Road & Rail Safety Guidelines
Source: IMD, NHAI, Ministry of Railways Fog Safety SOPs

## IMD Fog Classification by Visibility
| Visibility | Category | Road Advisory |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| 1000–3000 m | Mist | Drive normally; use dipped headlights |
| 500–999 m | Shallow Fog | Reduce speed by 20%; use fog lamps |
| 200–499 m | Moderate Fog | Reduce speed to 40 km/h on highways; hazard lights ON |
| 50–199 m | Dense Fog | Maximum 30 km/h; do NOT overtake; maximum caution |
| < 50 m | Very Dense Fog | STOP at safe location; emergency blinkers; do not drive |

## Fog Season in India
- **Peak Fog Belt**: Dec–Feb in IGP (Punjab → UP → Bihar → WB)
- **Worst Months**: December–January in Delhi, Agra, Lucknow, Patna, Varanasi
- **Daily Pattern**: Fog forms 3 AM – 9 AM; typically clears by 10–11 AM

## Train Fog Protocol (Indian Railways)
- Fog signal devices (detonators) placed on tracks in low-visibility zones
- Speed restriction: Max 60 km/h when visibility < 200 m; 40 km/h when < 100 m
- Linke Hofmann Busch (LHB) trains: Advanced fog-pass device scans track
- Typical cancellations: 50–100 trains/day during dense fog spells in Delhi division

## Aviation Fog Thresholds
- IFR: Visibility < 5 km OR ceiling < 1000 ft
- LIFR: Visibility < 1.6 km OR ceiling < 500 ft
- Delhi IGI (CAT IIIB certified): Operations to 50m RVR in very dense fog
- Smaller airports (CAT I only): Operations halt below 550m RVR
