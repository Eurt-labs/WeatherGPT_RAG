# AQI Health Advisory — India (CPCB & WHO Standards)
Source: CPCB National Air Quality Index, WHO Air Quality Guidelines 2021

## India CPCB AQI Categories (24-hour PM2.5 based)
| AQI Range | Category | Colour | PM2.5 (µg/m³) | Health Message |
|-----------|---------|--------|---------------|----------------|
| 0–50 | Good | Green | 0–30 | Air quality is satisfactory; minimal risk |
| 51–100 | Satisfactory | Light Green | 31–60 | Minor discomfort for sensitive people |
| 101–200 | Moderate | Yellow | 61–90 | Sensitive groups (asthma, heart) should limit outdoor activity |
| 201–300 | Poor | Orange | 91–120 | General public may experience effects; avoid prolonged outdoor exertion |
| 301–400 | Very Poor | Red | 121–250 | Respiratory impact on healthy people; serious health effects for sensitive groups |
| 401–500 | Severe | Dark Red/Maroon | > 250 | Health emergency; everyone should avoid outdoor activities |

| City | Worst Month(s) | Primary Pollutants | Key Causes |
|------|---------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Delhi NCR | Nov–Jan | PM2.5, PM10, NO₂ | Stubble burning (Oct-Nov) + winter inversion |
| Mumbai | Dec–Feb | PM10, O₃ | Vehicular emissions + sea breeze stagnation |
| Kolkata | Nov–Dec | PM2.5, SO₂ | Industrial emissions + coal burning |
| Bengaluru | Jan, Mar | PM10, NO₂ | Construction dust + vehicular |
| Kanpur/Agra | Oct–Jan | PM2.5 | Biomass burning + dust |

## Health Advisory by AQI
- AQI > 200: Wear N95/P100 mask outdoors; keep windows closed; avoid exercise
- AQI > 300: Children, elderly, pregnant women should remain indoors
- AQI > 400: Schools typically closed (CPCB GRAP Stage III in Delhi)
- GRAP Stage IV (Delhi only): Triggered at AQI > 450; ban on diesel vehicles, construction

## Commuter Specific Advice
- Cycling/walking: Avoid if AQI > 150 (moderate/poor); use N95 mask
- Car users: Recirculate cabin air (A/C on recirculation) when AQI > 200
- Children school run: If AQI > 300 at 7–9 AM, recommend mask use
